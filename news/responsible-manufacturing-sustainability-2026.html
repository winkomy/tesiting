Our Commitment to a More Sustainable Future

SUSTAINABILITY · UNPUBLISHED

At WINKO, we believe responsible manufacturing is an important part of building a more sustainable future.

As a water storage tank manufacturer, we continue to improve our production practices with greater attention to material efficiency, waste reduction, responsible resource use and a cleaner working environment.

Our approach includes making better use of production materials, reducing unnecessary waste and encouraging the proper recycling and management of metal off-cuts and other manufacturing materials.

We also recognize the important role reliable water storage infrastructure plays in supporting responsible water management for buildings, industries and communities.

Sustainability is an ongoing process. WINKO will continue reviewing our operations and identifying practical ways to improve efficiency while maintaining the quality, durability and reliability expected from our water storage solutions.
