JKR Visit to FRP Tank Factory

FACTORY VISIT

WINKO was pleased to welcome representatives from Jabatan Kerja Raya (JKR) for a visit to the FRP water tank manufacturing facility.

The visit provided an opportunity to observe the manufacturing environment, review FRP water tank production processes and exchange technical knowledge relating to water storage solutions.

Factory visits such as this allow closer communication between industry professionals and provide valuable opportunities to share practical manufacturing experience.

We appreciate the representatives from JKR for taking the time to visit the facility.
