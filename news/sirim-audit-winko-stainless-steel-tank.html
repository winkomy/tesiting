SIRIM Audit on WINKO Stainless Steel Tank

QUALITY ASSURANCE

WINKO recently underwent a SIRIM audit involving our stainless steel water tank system.

The audit included inspection and assessment activities relating to the product and manufacturing process as part of our ongoing commitment to quality assurance and consistent manufacturing standards.

Our team supported the inspection process by providing access to the tank, relevant manufacturing information and the required product measurements.

Quality control and continuous improvement remain important parts of WINKO's manufacturing operations.
