KPKT Officers Visit WINKO Factory

FACTORY VISIT

WINKO was pleased to welcome officers from the Ministry of Housing and Local Government, Kementerian Perumahan dan Kerajaan Tempatan (KPKT), for a factory visit.

The visit provided an opportunity for our team to introduce WINKO's manufacturing operations, water storage solutions and production capabilities.

The programme also included discussions and knowledge sharing between the visiting officers and the WINKO team.

We appreciate the KPKT officers for taking the time to visit our facility and engage with our team.
