Product overview

Official WINKO product information

The FRP Panel Tank is manufactured using Glass Fiber Reinforced Plastic panels. It is suitable for projects that require non-metallic material, corrosion resistance, modular installation, and documented technical compliance.

  • Glass Fiber Reinforced Plastic panel construction
  • Lightweight compared with steel tanks
  • Strong corrosion resistance
  • Modular design for custom tank sizing
  • Suitable for technical submissions and project / tender applications
