Responsible Water Storage

WINKO’s sustainability content focuses on material selection, product life, and responsible water storage solutions. Claims below remain scoped to the applicable material or product family.

01

Food-Grade Quality

Food-grade material options are identified for applicable products, including food-grade polyethylene in the POLYSTOR range and selected stainless steel applications.
