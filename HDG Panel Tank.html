What is an HDG panel tank?

An HDG panel tank is a modular water storage tank assembled from pressed mild-steel panels protected by a hot-dip galvanised coating. The sectional construction allows the tank to be configured for different project capacities and installation conditions while providing corrosion protection for long-term service.

Typical WINKO HDG panel tank applications

  • Commercial buildings and factories
  • Apartments, schools and institutions
  • Industrial water storage
  • Fire protection water storage
  • General building water supply systems

Why specify a hot-dip galvanised sectional tank?

The WINKO HDG system combines pressed-steel panel strength, modular site assembly and a hot-dip galvanised finish. This makes it suitable where project teams need a sectional steel water tank with practical corrosion protection and the flexibility to suit the required tank arrangement.
