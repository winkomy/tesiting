Product overview

Official WINKO product information

The HDPE Tank is best positioned as a heavier-duty non-metallic tank option where chemical resistance, corrosion resistance, and industrial durability are important. Final suitability must be confirmed based on the stored liquid, concentration, temperature, and project design.
