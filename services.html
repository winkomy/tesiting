How we help

Services for your water storage needs.

Whether you need a new installation, expert maintenance, or refurbishment, our team of professionals is here to meet all your needs.

01 / Maintenance

Water Tank Maintenance

Regular maintenance is essential for keeping your Pressed Steel Tank, Bolted Steel Tank, Hot Dipped Tank, Sprinkle Tank, or Hose Reel Tank in top condition. Our expert team performs thorough inspections and maintenance to ensure your tank operates efficiently and lasts longer.

Our maintenance services include:

  • Routine Inspections: Checking for potential issues, leaks, and wear in your water tank, whether it’s a Pressed Steel Tank or a Hose Reel Tank.
  • Cleaning & Degassing: Removing contaminants and ensuring the internal condition of the tank, such as in Sprinkle Tanks or Bolted Steel Tanks, is optimal.
  • Component Checks: Ensuring all parts, such as valves and seals, are working effectively, especially in Hot Dipped Tanks or Hose Reel Tanks.
02 / Repair

Water Tank Repair & Refurbishment

If your Pressed Steel Tank, Bolted Steel Tank, Hot Dipped Tank, Sprinkle Tank, or Hose Reel Tank has experienced wear, damage, or age-related deterioration, we offer expert repair and refurbishment services. We restore your tank to full functionality, minimizing downtime and maximizing its lifespan.

Services include:

  • Leak Repairs: Fixing cracks, leaks, or any structural damage in your Sprinkle Tank or Pressed Steel Tank.
  • Component Replacement: Replacing worn-out parts such as seals, valves, and fittings in Bolted Steel Tanks or Hot Dipped Tanks.
  • Refurbishment: Updating older tanks to meet modern standards for efficiency and safety, whether it’s a Sprinkle Tank or Hose Reel Tank.
03 / Cleaning

Water Tank Cleaning

Keeping your water tank clean is crucial for maintaining water quality. Whether you have a Pressed Steel Tank, Bolted Steel Tank, Hot Dipped Tank, Sprinkle Tank, or Hose Reel Tank, we offer professional water tank cleaning services to ensure that your tank is free from debris, sediments, and contaminants, thus providing clean and safe water.

Our cleaning process involves:

  • Drainage & Removal: Emptying the tank and removing debris or dirt from Pressed Steel Tanks or Hose Reel Tanks.
  • Deep Cleaning: Using specialized tools to scrub and sanitize the interior of Sprinkle Tanks or Bolted Steel Tanks.
  • Disinfection: Ensuring the tank is free of harmful bacteria and contaminants, especially in Hot Dipped Tanks.
04 / Consultation

Water Tank Consultation Services

Not sure which type of tank or service is right for you? Our consultation services provide expert guidance to help you make informed decisions based on your specific needs. Whether you’re installing a Pressed Steel Tank, maintaining an existing Bolted Steel Tank, or upgrading your Hot Dipped Tank, we are here to assist.

Consultation services include:

  • Site Assessment: Evaluating your needs and recommending the most appropriate tank solution, whether it’s a Sprinkle Tank or a Hose Reel Tank.
  • System Design: Offering advice on sizing, configuration, and tank materials, including the best options for Pressed Steel Tanks or Hot Dipped Tanks.
  • Ongoing Support: Providing guidance on maintenance schedules, upgrades, and tank care, specifically for Sprinkle Tanks and Hose Reel Tanks.
