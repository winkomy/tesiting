Product overview

Official WINKO product information

The Stainless Steel Panel Tank is designed for clients who need better material quality, cleaner appearance, and stronger long-term corrosion resistance than standard mild steel tanks. It is suitable for high-demand commercial, industrial, and clean-water projects.

  • SUS304 or SUS316 stainless steel construction
  • Excellent corrosion resistance
  • Hydraulically pressed modular panel design
  • Premium finish and clean appearance
  • Suitable for demanding water storage applications
Tank complianceSANS 10328:2020
MaterialSUS304 or SUS316 stainless steel
