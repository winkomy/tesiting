HDG Panel Tank + HDPE Lining

Product overview

Official WINKO product information

This system uses a galvanised steel panel structure with an internal HDPE lining. It is suitable when the project requires steel strength but also needs an internal protective barrier to reduce direct water contact with the steel surface.

  • HDG panel tank external structure
  • Internal HDPE lining for extra protection
  • Useful for leakage-control and corrosion-sensitive projects
  • Suitable as an upgrade from standard bolted steel tanks
  • Can be customized to project drawings and connection requirements
Tank structureHot-dip galvanised pressed mild steel panel tank
Internal liningHDPE lining as protective internal barrier
External coatingHDG coating typically conforming to EN ISO 1461 when supplied under HDG pressed steel specification
